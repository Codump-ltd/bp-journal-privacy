Privacy Policy for BP Journal

Last updated: April 6, 2026

BP Journal is designed to help users track blood pressure readings.

1. Information We Collect

BP Journal stores the information you enter, including:

  • Patient names
  • Blood pressure readings (systolic/diastolic)
  • Pulse values
  • Timestamps of entries

2. How Data Is Stored

Data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. BP Journal does not require account creation and does not upload your health data to our servers.

3. Data Sharing

BP Journal does not automatically share your data with third parties. If you use the in-app share feature (for example, WhatsApp), data is shared only based on your explicit action.

4. Permissions

BP Journal only uses permissions necessary for app functionality (for example, opening external apps for sharing).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may delete locally stored app data depending on your device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

BP Journal is not directed to children under 13.

7. Medical Disclaimer

BP Journal is a tracking tool only and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
